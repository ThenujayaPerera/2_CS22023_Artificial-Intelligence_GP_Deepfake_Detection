# 8. Approach 3 — Transfer learning

This is the approach I would seriously consider for your **final model**.

Instead of designing hundreds of convolutional layers ourselves:

```text
Your image
     ↓
Pretrained CNN
     ↓
Feature extraction
     ↓
Your classifier
     ↓
REAL / FAKE
```

Possible architectures include:

* EfficientNet
* ResNet
* ConvNeXt
* Xception

For your first serious experiment, I'd use **EfficientNet**.

Why?

Because you have a large dataset, and transfer learning gives you a strong starting feature extractor while still allowing you to fine-tune it for your particular face dataset.

---

# 9. Approach 4 — Fine-tuning

Transfer learning doesn't have to stop at:

```text
Pretrained model
      ↓
Frozen
      ↓
Classifier
```

We can eventually do:

```text
Pretrained model
      ↓
Train classifier
      ↓
Unfreeze selected deeper layers
      ↓
Train with small learning rate
```

This is called **fine-tuning**.

For example:

```text
EfficientNet

████████████████████
↑ frozen            ↑ fine-tuned
```

Then the model can adapt its features to deepfake artifacts.

# 22. Dataset split

Ideally:

```text
200,000

TRAIN
80%
≈ 160,000

VALIDATION
10%
≈ 20,000

TEST
10%
≈ 20,000
```

But there is a very important issue:

### Identity leakage

If the same person's face appears in training and testing:

```text
TRAIN
Person A

TEST
Person A
```

your test score can be misleading.

We want:

```text
TRAIN
Person A
Person B
Person C
...

VALIDATION
Person X
Person Y
...

TEST
Person Z
Person W
...
```

If your dataset doesn't contain identity information, we'll need to inspect what metadata or naming structure it provides and use the best leakage-control strategy available.

# 27. Evaluation

After training:

```text
Test images
      ↓
Model
      ↓
Predictions
      ↓
REAL / FAKE
```

Calculate:

### Accuracy

```text
Correct / Total
```

### Precision

```text
Predicted fake
        ↓
How many actually fake?
```

### Recall

```text
Actual fake
     ↓
How many detected?
```

### F1

Balances precision and recall.

### ROC-AUC

Measures the model's ability to distinguish the two classes across thresholds.
